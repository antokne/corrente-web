Input 1 — Generation

What your system makes

Set how much energy your panels produce each year.

Input 2 — Time

Over how many years

Look ahead up to 30 years.

Input 3 — Self-consumption

How much you use yourself

Energy you use at home is worth more than energy you export, so this has the biggest effect on your return.

Input 4 — Import rate

Your grid rate

What each unit you use yourself is worth — the price you'd have paid the grid.

Input 5 — Export rate

Your feed-in rate

What you earn for the surplus you send back.
