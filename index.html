Why it's different

Costs from real usage, not estimates.

Most energy apps read a single meter and estimate the rest. Corrente takes real, per-circuit usage from your Basis switchboard and accounts for the energy your own solar and battery supplied — so every kilowatt-hour is priced against the grid rate you would have paid, using your Basis retail plan's own peak, shoulder, and off-peak bands.

  • Per-circuit usage straight from the Basis switchboard
  • Solar & battery attributed before any kWh is billed to the grid
  • Retail plan rates across peak, shoulder, and off-peak
  • Export credits & amortised costs for solar and battery capital
What it does

Features

Live

Your home energy at a glance — an animated hub-and-spoke view of solar generation, battery charge and discharge, grid import and export, and home consumption in real time.

Energy History

Hourly, daily, monthly, and yearly bar charts with solar, battery, and grid attribution — see where your energy came from.

Energy Costs

A stacked cost breakdown with your retail plan's peak, shoulder, and off-peak bands, standing charges, export credits, and amortised solar and battery costs.

Solar ROI

Return on investment for your solar PV system over its lifetime — total generation and export with an estimated payback period and remaining balance.

Switchboard

Per-circuit energy history and a ranked consumption list from your Basis switchboard — see which of your active circuits are drawing the most power.

Subcircuit Drill-In

Tap into any circuit for its own time-series with period navigation, from a single day out to the full year.
