Why it's different

Your switchboard can't tell solar from grid.

Behind a backup gateway, it all arrives as one supply. Corrente works out which circuits ran on solar and battery, and prices the rest at your plan's rates.

How circuit costs work →

  • It measures. Every circuit, live, from your Basis switchboard.
  • It allocates. Solar and battery go to each circuit in proportion to what it drew.
  • It prices. Grid power at your plan's peak, shoulder, and off-peak rates.
  • It counts. Export credits, plus the amortised cost of your solar and battery.

What it does

Everything in one app.

Home

Watch power flow between solar, battery, grid, and home, live. Your switchboard summary, battery level, and grid status sit just below.

Energy History

See where every kilowatt-hour came from. View solar, home use, battery, and grid by hour, day, or month.

Energy Costs

See what you paid, and what your solar saved. Costs split into peak, shoulder, and off-peak, with your bill next to the full cost.

Savings & Solar ROI

Watch your system pay itself back. Track what your solar and battery save compared with buying from the grid.

Live Switchboard

Every circuit's power, voltage, current, and share of the load, live. Rename a circuit once and the name follows it through the app.

Per-Circuit Detail

Find the circuit behind the bill. See which circuits use the most and what each one costs, plus breaker, RCD, arc-fault, and temperature status in plain language.

Automations

Let your home react on its own. Start from a template and build rules on time, battery level, or free-power periods. Rules can set your battery reserve and mode, or turn circuits off and back on.
