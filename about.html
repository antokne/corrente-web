About

Corrente is developed by Ant G, an ex-Wahooligan who helped build the Wahoo ELEMNT iOS Companion app. Corrente grew out of wanting one honest view of a home's energy — where it's generated, where it's stored, and what it actually costs.

Corrente is Italian for current — the flow of electricity, and of energy through your home.
